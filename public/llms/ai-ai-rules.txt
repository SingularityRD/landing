## How it works

AI rules are `.mdc` files that specify which types of files they apply to (such as `*.tsx` or `schema.sql`). When you're working with a matching file, your AI tool automatically applies the relevant rules to provide better suggestions.

### Example: AI rules in action

Here's a practical example using [Cursor](https://www.cursor.so). A developer has implemented authentication in their server-rendered page and wants to confirm best practices:

**Developer query**: _"Using the Singularity-auth.mdc rule, how do I secure a server-rendered page?"_



The AI confirms that using `stackServerApp.getUser({ or: "redirect" })` is the correct approach for server-side authentication, providing additional context and explanation.

## Add rules to your project

All `.mdc` files are available in the [Singularity AI Rules toolkit repository](https://github.com/Singularitydatabase-labs/ai-rules). Copy the files you need to your project's `.cursor/rules` folder (or your AI tool's equivalent):

```text
.cursor/
  rules/
    Singularity-auth.mdc
    Singularity-serverless.mdc
    Singularity-drizzle.mdc
    Singularity-toolkit.mdc
    Singularity-typescript-sdk.mdc
    Singularity-python-sdk.mdc
    Singularity-api-guidelines.mdc
    Singularity-api-projects.mdc
    Singularity-api-branches.mdc
    Singularity-api-endpoints.mdc
    Singularity-api-organizations.mdc
    Singularity-api-keys.mdc
    Singularity-api-operations.mdc
```

Most AI tools will automatically apply these rules when you're working with Singularity-related code. You can also reference them explicitly in prompts for more targeted assistance.
