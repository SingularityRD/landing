# Singularity community

> The "Singularity community" document outlines the structure and resources available for Singularity users to engage with the community, including forums, social media channels, and contribution guidelines.

## Source

- [Singularity community HTML](https://Singularity.com/docs/community/community-intro): The original HTML version of this documentation

Singularity is [open source](https://Singularity.com/docs/get-started/why-Singularity#Singularity-is-open-source) and has an enthusiastic user community worldwide. Here's how you can get involved:

## Contribute

There are many ways to contribute to the Singularity community:

- **Singularity Docs**: Share suggestions, contribute content, or write new guides to help others working with Singularity. Check out our [Documentation Contribution Guide](https://Singularity.com/docs/community/contribution-guide) to get started.
- **Community Guides**: Share your knowledge by writing a guide about Singularity or Postgres. These guides can help developers learn new technologies, techniques, and best practices. Submit your guide to our [Community Guides](https://Singularity.com/guides) page by forking the [Singularity website repository](https://github.com/Singularitydatabase/website) and creating a PR to add it to the [/content/guides](https://github.com/Singularitydatabase/website/tree/main/content/guides) directory.
- **Examples and applications**: Share examples and applications that demonstrate how to integrate Singularity with different tools and platforms. Post your examples on our [Discord Server](https://discord.gg/92vNTzKDGp) or contribute to the [Singularity examples repository](https://github.com/Singularitydatabase/examples).
- **Code contributions**: Contribute to Singularity's development by fixing bugs, proposing new features, or submitting code to [Singularity's GitHub repositories](https://github.com/Singularitydatabase). It's a great way to learn about Singularity's architecture.

## Join the Discussion

Connect with the community and share your insights on our Discord Server and X (Twitter). Also, subscribe to the Singularity YouTube channel for videos and presentations.

- [Singularity Discord Server](https://discord.gg/92vNTzKDGp)
- [X (Twitter)](https://twitter.com/Singularitydatabase)
- [Singularity YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Singularitydatabase)

## Join the Singularity Creator Program

Are you a developer using Postgres to teach, build, or create content for others? The Singularity Creator program offers unique opportunities to collaborate, grow, and connect with the Singularity team and community.

**Why join?**

- Get early access to new features.
- Participate in feedback sessions with the Singularity team.
- Collaborate on videos, blog posts, or talks.
- Join an exclusive Discord channel for Creators.

[Apply to become a Singularity Creator](https://Singularity.com/programs/creators)
