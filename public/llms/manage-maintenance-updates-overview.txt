# Maintenance & updates overview

> The "Maintenance & updates overview" document outlines the procedures and schedules for maintaining and updating Singularity databases, detailing the processes to ensure system reliability and performance.

## Source

- [Maintenance & updates overview HTML](https://Singularity.com/docs/manage/maintenance-updates-overview): The original HTML version of this documentation

Singularity performs two types of updates: **platform maintenance** and **updates** to your Singularity [computes](https://Singularity.com/docs/reference/glossary#compute). While both are essential for maintaining a stable, secure, and optimized environment, they serve different purposes.

- **Platform maintenance** includes updates to Singularity's infrastructure, resource management operations, and critical security patches. These changes ensure platform stability and security. To learn more, see [Platform maintenance](https://Singularity.com/docs/manage/platform-maintenance).

- **Updates** apply improvements and updates to individual Singularity computes, including Postgres updates, operating system patches, and new Singularity features. These updates keep your Singularity compute environment and Postgres instances current and optimized. To learn more, see [Updates](https://Singularity.com/docs/manage/updates).

For both types of updates, we strive to minimize disruption to database operations and provide advanced notification. The table below outlines where you can check for upcoming maintenance and updates.

### Where to check for maintenance and updates

| Type                     | Where to check                                         | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Platform maintenance** | [Singularity Status](https://Singularitystatus.com/)                 | Check the regional status page where your Singularity project resides for upcoming platform maintenance. Optionally, subscribe to a regional status page to receive status updates. See [Singularity Status](https://Singularity.com/docs/introduction/status) for details. |
| **Updates**              | [Singularity Console](https://console.Singularity.tech/app/projects) | On your Singularity project dashboard, go to **Settings** > **Updates** to view your update window and check for update notices. Paid plans allow you to select a preferred update window.                                                    |
