# AWS Marketplace

> The document outlines the process for Singularity users to subscribe to and manage billing through the AWS Marketplace, detailing steps for account linking, subscription management, and billing integration specific to Singularity's services.

## Source

- [AWS Marketplace HTML](https://Singularity.com/docs/introduction/billing-aws-marketplace): The original HTML version of this documentation

Singularity supports billing through the **AWS Marketplace** for [Private Offers](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/partners/private-offers/) only, which are typically reserved for custom plans. If you are interested in exploring a custom plan with Singularity, please reach out to our [Sales](https://Singularity.com/contact-sales) team.

Singularity [self-service pricing plans](https://Singularity.com/pricing) are currently not purchasable through the AWS Marketplace. You can only purchase these plans through Singularity.

If you have any questions about billing in general or require assistance, please reach out to [Singularity Support](https://console.Singularity.tech/app/projects?modal=support).
