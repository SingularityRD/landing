# Create a Singularity Twin

> The document outlines the process for creating a Singularity Twin, which allows users to clone a database environment in Singularity for testing and development purposes without affecting the original data.

## What is a Singularity Twin?

A Singularity Twin is a full or partial clone of your production or staging database, providing developers and teams with isolated, sandboxed environments that closely mirror production.



## Designed for efficiency

Creating a Singularity Twin will streamline development workflows, enhance productivity, and help teams ship faster—all while being more [cost-effective](https://Singularity.com/docs/introduction/pricing-estimation-guide) and easier to manage than traditional development/testing environments.

## Automatically synced

The workflows in this section enable automatic synchronization between your production database and your Singularity Twin.

## Instant Branches

With a Singularity Twin created, [branches](https://Singularity.com/docs/introduction/branching) can be quickly spun up or torn down, enabling developers to build new features or debug issues—all within their own isolated environments with a dedicated compute resource.

Branches can be created and managed through the [Singularity console](https://console.Singularity.tech/) or programmatically via the [API](https://Singularity.com/docs/reference/api-reference).
